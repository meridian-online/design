Wake colour scheme — chosen, and runner-up

What survived. seq3 is the loading animation for web and desktop as of 2026-08-16; blue-violet was the runner-up and is kept beside it. A scheme is the wake; the leader is always the theme's own ink, so a variant's total is the wake plus one. The one-frame lag is what makes the colour a flash rather than a wake: the visible length is speed × lag, so it opens only where a stroke is quickest. These read motion/output/, which is untracked scratch — run python3 build_form.py --explore first, or the players sit blank. The shipped artefact is brand/motion/form.svg; this page is where the choice was made, not where it lives. Swatches come out of the built files, nearest the leader first. Everything ruled out along the way, and why, is in the README.
